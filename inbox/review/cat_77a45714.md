It’s a natural next step for Dalton, and one we’re excited to support. While he’s stepping back from day-to-day work at YC, he’ll remain part of the community as Partner Emeritus, continuing to be a resource to founders and alumni in a more limited capacity. We appreciate Dalton’s many contributions to YC over the past decade and look forward to cheering him on in this next chapter and beyond.
